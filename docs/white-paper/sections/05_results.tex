\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

We report estimates in the order readers should interpret them: naive TWFE, preferred full-panel Callaway--Sant'Anna (CS), matched-sample CS, then residualized CS.
All ATTs are differences in monthly rent index levels (\$/month) unless noted otherwise.

\paragraph{Naive TWFE (matched panel).}
The TWFE event study on the matched panel provides an intuitive first pass but remains vulnerable to staggered-treatment bias when effects differ across cohorts.
Comparison plots (\texttt{did\_twfe\_vs\_cs\_comparison.png}) typically show post-period gaps between TWFE and CS coefficients.

\paragraph{Preferred full-panel CS.}
The headline estimand is the pooled CS ATT on the \textbf{unmatched} rent panel (\texttt{cs\_overall\_att\_full\_panel}), estimated before trend matching.
Event-study dynamics for this sample appear in \texttt{did\_callaway\_santanna\_event\_study\_full\_panel.png}.
This specification uses all tracts with ZORI coverage and never-treated comparison units.

\paragraph{Matched-sample robustness.}
Restricting to $k{=}3$ neighbors matched on pre-trend slope and average pre-treatment rent yields a smaller but more parallel-trends-comparable panel (\texttt{did\_story\_matching\_love.png}, \texttt{did\_matching\_diagnostics.csv}).
The matched CS ATT (\texttt{cs\_overall\_att}) and event study (\texttt{did\_callaway\_santanna\_event\_study.png}) should be read as robustness to the full-panel headline, not as a substitute for it.

\paragraph{Residualized CS (non-comparable scale).}
Figure~\ref{fig:cs-controls-main} shows dynamic CS estimates after removing ACS covariates and tract-specific linear trends fit on pre-prohibition months.
Pre-trends often look smoother than raw matched or full-panel event studies, but residualized pooled ATTs must not be quoted as observed rent changes without scale translation.

\paragraph{Design-parameter sensitivity.}
Appendix summaries in \texttt{docs/robustness/k\_neighbors\_sweep\_summary.csv} and \texttt{threshold\_percentile\_sweep\_summary.csv} trace headline CS ATTs as matching depth and treatment-intensity cutoffs vary.
Treatment-definition sensitivity (\texttt{DID\_TREATMENT\_MODE=binary}) is reported in parallel output trees (\texttt{*-binary} vs \texttt{*-threshold}).
