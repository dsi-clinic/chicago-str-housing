\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

\paragraph{Staggered timing.}
Treatments arrive at different calendar times; na\"ive TWFE can reweight cohorts implicitly in ways that distort economic interpretation \citep{goodman-bacon2021difference}.

\paragraph{Estimator ladder.}
\textbf{(1) Naive TWFE.} On the trend-matched panel we estimate a conventional two-way fixed effects event study (\texttt{EventStudyAnalyzer}) with $\pm12$ pre- and $+36$ post-month windows.
\textbf{(2) Preferred full-panel CS.} Before matching, \texttt{CallawaySantAnnaAnalyzer} with \texttt{result\_suffix=\_full\_panel} pools all tracts with rent observations---this is the headline ATT for policy readers.
\textbf{(3) Matched-sample CS.} The same estimator on the matched panel restricts controls to $k$-NN neighbors matched on slope and level.
\textbf{(4) Residualized CS.} \texttt{CallawaySantAnnaWithControlsAnalyzer} removes tract-specific linear trends and ACS covariates fit on pre-treatment months only; magnitudes are \emph{not} directly comparable to raw rent ATTs.

\paragraph{Group--time ATT and event studies.}
Following \citet{callaway2021difference}, we summarize ATTs at different horizons relative to first prohibition, using never-treated units as the primary comparison group.
Doubly robust residualization follows \citet{santanna2020doubly}.
Optional tract-cluster block bootstrap and a joint Wald pre-trend test on baseline CS event-study coefficients (from the \texttt{controls} integration branch) supplement analytic standard errors when enabled via \texttt{CS\_BOOTSTRAP\_REPS}.

\paragraph{Robustness grids.}
We document sensitivity to $k \in \{1,\ldots,5\}$ and threshold percentiles $\{0.10, 0.15, 0.25, 0.33, 0.50\}$ in \texttt{docs/robustness/} (see \texttt{run\_robustness\_sweeps.py}).

\paragraph{Pipeline evidence layers.}
The script \texttt{housing.scripts.did\_pipeline\_callaway\_santanna} registers descriptive balance, story tables, temporal and spatial diagnostics, TWFE, CS, and whitepaper extras when \texttt{DID\_WHITEPAPER\_MODE=1}; see \texttt{docs/DID\_CS\_RUNBOOK.md} and \texttt{docs/PREFERRED\_SPEC.md}.
