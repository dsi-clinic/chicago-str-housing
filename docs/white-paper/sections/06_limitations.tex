\section{Limitations and threats to validity}\label{sec:limits}

\textbf{Design choices.} Headline estimates may move with the threshold percentile or number of matched controls; we report grids in \texttt{docs/robustness/}. Highly sensitive ranges should temper causal claims.

\textbf{Measurement.} ZIP-indexed rents only approximate intra-tract composition and may attenuate heterogeneous unit-level shocks; Zillow coverage gaps require interpolation flagged in preprocessing logs.

\textbf{Parallel trends.}
Even with tract-linear trends residualized away on pre-ban months (\texttt{CallawaySantAnnaWithControlsAnalyzer}), curvature or macro shocks violating linearity threaten interpretation; pretrend regressions exported from \texttt{PretrendDiagnosticAnalyzer} quantify residual slope differences but cannot replace substantive economic modeling of neighborhood amenity shocks (see narrative in \texttt{docs/PRETREND\_VIOLATIONS\_AND\_FIXES.md}).

\textbf{Spillovers.}
Spatial spillovers (\emph{e.g.,} Airbnb demand hopping across boundaries) attenuate contrasts toward zero despite policy bite; exploratory synthetic-control prototypes (\texttt{docs/exploratory/SYNTHETIC\_PROTOTYPE\_NOTES.md}) may illuminate narrow comparisons but inherit different identification logic than broader staggered estimators.

\textbf{Interpretation.}
Double/debiased machine-learning estimators orthogonalize high-dimensional nuisance terms under distinct asymptotics (\citealp{chernozhukov2018double}) and belong in methodological appendices, not headline tables, unless staggered timelines are painstakingly reconstructed.
